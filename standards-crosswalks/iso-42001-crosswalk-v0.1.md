## 1. Supportive Alignment Scope & Conformity Disclaimer

1. **Supportive Technical Alignment Only**: Implementing CIAF-LCM v0.1 provides **supportive technical controls and cryptographic evidentiary artifacts** aligned with selected clauses (`Clauses 4–10`) and Annex A controls of `ISO/IEC 42001:2023`.
2. **No Claim of Full Certification Conformity**: `ISO/IEC 42001:2023` is an organizational **Management System Standard (MSS)** audited by accredited certification bodies across governance leadership, stakeholder context, AI impact assessments, competence, internal audit charters, management reviews, and continual improvement. **Technical runtime controls alone NEVER satisfy ISO/IEC 42001:2023 certification conformity.** Every mapping below explicitly states the organizational and procedural gaps that an institution `MUST` satisfy outside CIAF-LCM.

| **Clause 6 — Planning** (`6.1.2` AI risk assessment, `6.1.3` AI risk treatment, `6.1.4` AI system impact assessment, `6.2` AI objectives) | `CIAF-01`, `CIAF-04`, `CIAF-06`, `CIAF-07` | `UseCaseRecord.risk_tier`; delegation thresholds (`autonomous_max_amount`, `escalation_max_amount`); Statement of Applicability (SoA) trace to `CIAF-01`–`CIAF-12`. | **Partial (Risk Treatment Implementation)** — Implements deterministic pre-execution risk treatment (`ALLOW`, `DENY`, `ESCALATE`, `INSPECT`). *Gap*: Pre-deployment societal/individual AI System Impact Assessments (`6.1.4`) and enterprise AI KPIs (`6.2`) are procedural artifacts outside CIAF-LCM. |
| **Clause 7 — Support** (`7.1` Resources, `7.2` Competence, `7.3` Awareness, `7.4` Communication, `7.5` Documented information) | `CIAF-05`, `CIAF-07`, `CIAF-09` | Versioned `policy_snapshot`; `authorized_human_reviewers`; immutable hash-chained `AuditCapsule` (`7.5.3` control of documented information). | **Supportive (`7.5` Documented Information Integrity)** — Provides RFC 8785 + Ed25519 tamper-evident protection for operational governance records. *Gap*: Staff training/competence records (`7.2`), awareness campaigns (`7.3`), and external communication plans (`7.4`) are out of scope. |
| **Clause 8 — Operation** (`8.1` Operational planning & control, `8.2` AI risk assessment execution, `8.3` AI risk treatment execution, `8.4` AI system impact assessment) | `CIAF-03`, `CIAF-06`, `CIAF-07`, `CIAF-08`, `CIAF-11`, `CIAF-12` | Gateway `AuthorizationClaim`; `<= 30s` single-use `ExecutionGrant`; Executor `ExecutionClaim` (`SUCCEEDED`, `NOT_ATTEMPTED_GATEWAY_BLOCKED`, or `REJECTED_SECURITY_EXCEPTION`). | **Direct (Runtime Operational Control for Tool Execution)** — Directly enforces and records operational risk treatment at runtime. *Gap*: Data acquisition governance, model training/fine-tuning pipelines, and periodic impact reassessments (`8.4`) require separate MLOps/governance controls. |
| **Clause 9 — Performance Evaluation** (`9.1` Monitoring, measurement, analysis & evaluation, `9.2` Internal audit, `9.3` Management review) | `CIAF-09`, `CIAF-10` | Stateless `IndependentVerifier` outputting `AssuranceClaim`; 2-Axis Readiness Scorecard (`Control Maturity` vs. `Evidence Confidence`). | **Partial (`9.1` & `9.2` Technical Audit Evidence)** — Equips internal and external auditors with zero-trust capsule verification and adversarial test evidence. *Gap*: Internal auditor independence charter (`9.2`) and executive Management Review minutes (`9.3`) are organizational processes. |
| **Clause 10 — Improvement** (`10.1` Continual improvement, `10.2` Nonconformity and corrective action) | `CIAF-10`, `CIAF-11`, `CIAF-12` | `ExecutionClaim.security_exception` (`CIAF-11`); `AssuranceClaim.failed_controls` & `findings`; immediate `CIAF-12` revocation records. | **Supportive (`10.2` Immediate Containment & Root-Cause Evidence)** — Contains runtime nonconformities immediately and preserves signed forensic evidence. *Gap*: Root-cause analysis (RCA) ticketing, CAPA tracking, and systemic preventive process updates reside in GRC/ITSM tooling. |
